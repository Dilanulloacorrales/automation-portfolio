# Recruiter Start Here

This page is a short guide for reviewing this portfolio in about five minutes.

## Who I am
I am a developer specializing in process automation for payroll, accounting, and reconciliation. My work focuses on replacing manual, repetitive tasks with reliable, auditable workflows.

## What to look at first
1. **[Portfolio index](./README.md):** a table of all projects, with status and measured results.
2. **Two or three project folders:** each one describes the problem, the solution, and the result. Good starting points are *Canada Payroll Taxes Posting* and *Net Pay US Analysis*.
3. **Results:** the time savings are estimates from the business process, not guarantees.

## How I approach automation
- **Standardize first:** define the data and the steps before automating them.
- **Automate second:** build the tool once the process is consistent.
- **Build in controls:** include validation, traceability, and error checks from the start.
- **Keep improving:** review the tool after it goes live.
